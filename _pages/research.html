Better super-resolution imaging through optical hardware and computation.

I combine illumination engineering, interferometric detection, and computational inference to push microscopy in three complementary directions: finer spatial detail, faster acquisition and richer information, and more faithful reconstruction.

Higher resolution and localization precision

By designing how fluorescence is excited and detected, I seek to extract more spatial information from each measurement. My work spans scanning-free interferometric morphology imaging, three-beam 4Pi-SIM, and modulation-enhanced single-molecule localization.

  • Scanning-free 4Pi super-resolution morphology imaging. Fluorescence interference and structured illumination enable approximately 20 nm axial precision and 100 nm lateral resolution without axial scanning. Advanced Photonics (2023)
  • Three-beam 4Pi-SIM. Image interference structured illumination and spatial-spectral optimization improve axial resolution to approximately 150 nm. Laser & Photonics Reviews (2025)
  • SMILE & isoFLUX. Structured illumination strengthens 3D single-molecule localization, with sub-5 nm localization precision in real experiments. SMILE · Preprint (2025) · isoFLUX · Chinese Optics Letters (2025)

Faster imaging and more information dimensions

Spatial detail is only part of the picture. I also work on reducing the measurements needed for super-resolution and extending the information recovered from fluorescence, from rapid structural dynamics to molecular dipole orientation.

  • Deep learning for single-frame SIM. A single-frame reconstruction approach supports approximately 100 nm super-resolution at more than 100 frames per second, helping capture fast live-cell dynamics. APL Photonics (2024)
  • Polarization-resolved interferometric SIM. Building on the 2023 Advanced Photonics work, polarized structured illumination adds dipole-orientation information to high-axial-accuracy morphology measurements, enabling 6D imaging. ACS Photonics (2025)

Higher reconstruction fidelity

Accurate images require both a realistic forward model and an efficient inverse solution. I develop methods that account for optical aberrations, accelerate iterative reconstruction, suppress noise artifacts, and extract localization information close to its statistical limit.

  • In situ aberration estimation and correction. Vectorial PSF retrieval estimates optical aberrations directly from single-molecule data to improve 3D localization. Photonics Research (2024)
  • Faster spatial-domain 3D-SIM reconstruction. Nonlinear gradient descent accelerates iterative reconstruction. Optics Express (2021)
  • Fast reconstruction with noise regularization. TV-FISTA-SIM combines accelerated iteration with total-variation regularization to reduce noise artifacts and improve fidelity. Optics Express (2022)
  • Statistically efficient localization. An optimal fitting strategy for modulated illumination localization microscopy reaches the Cramér–Rao lower bound (CRLB) under the evaluated conditions. SPIE Conference (2023)
